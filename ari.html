01 Project introduction

Understand more.
Act with intention.

Ari is a financial technology project being developed by Buildify. Its purpose is to bring financial learning and market tools together so people can study markets, understand risk, and review their decisions in one considered experience.

The aim is to help people educate themselves, analyze markets, invest thoughtfully, and trade with a plan rather than blindly. Ari is not presented as an investment adviser and cannot promise profits or financial success.

02 Planned capabilities

Tools for a more
thoughtful process.

These are planned areas of Ari. They are not represented as finished or currently available features.

01PLANNED

Trading Journal

Record each trade and review the reasoning behind it. Planned records include:

  • Trade entries, screenshots, entry and exit prices
  • Stop loss, take profit, and risk-reward ratio
  • Notes on trading mistakes and lessons learned
  • Performance tracking over time
02PLANNED

Market Analysis

Study price behavior with tools intended to support a user's own analysis:

  • Market charts and price movement
  • Trend analysis and market structure
  • Liquidity concepts for learning and review
03PLANNED

Financial Calculations

Make common trade and investment calculations easier to inspect:

  • Profit and loss, including percentage returns
  • Risk calculations and position sizing
  • Risk-reward calculations before a trade
04PLANNED

Trading Statistics

Review activity and outcomes to better understand performance:

  • Total trades, winning trades, and losing trades
  • Win rate, average profit, and average loss
  • Trading performance charts and summaries
05PLANNED

Investment Education

Support self-guided learning with material about financial foundations:

  • Financial learning and investment basics
  • Risk management principles
  • Responsible, informed decision-making
06PLANNED

Market News

A planned feature to display relevant financial and cryptocurrency market news in one place. Sources, coverage, and availability will depend on future development.
